LEAD
A wrapped Monero token on Hyperliquid loses more than 99 percent after its deployer key leaks.

Wagyu's XMR1 fell from about $500 to around $1 in roughly half an hour on 20 November 2026 after an attacker sold tokens worth $13.5 million, according to Bitquery data reported by Decrypt. Wagyu's founder blames a breach at an Icelandic hosting company that has since shut down every service it runs.

XMR1 is a token that Wagyu issues on Hyperliquid to represent Monero, which cannot trade there natively, Decrypt reports. [https://decrypt.co/383732/wrapped-monero-token-collapses-99-after-13-million-hack-blamed-on-web-host] Native Monero was not affected. [https://decrypt.co/383732/wrapped-monero-token-collapses-99-after-13-million-hack-blamed-on-web-host]

According to Bitquery's analysis, cited by Decrypt, the deployer account sent 5.2 million XMR1 to a newly created account early on 20 November 2026. [https://decrypt.co/383732/wrapped-monero-token-collapses-99-after-13-million-hack-blamed-on-web-host] That account sold about 364,000 of them, roughly 7 percent of the transfer, to 388 buyers for $13.5 million. [https://decrypt.co/383732/wrapped-monero-token-collapses-99-after-13-million-hack-blamed-on-web-host] The proceeds became ETH, and about 7,350 ETH sat in three wallets at Bitquery's last check. [https://decrypt.co/383732/wrapped-monero-token-collapses-99-after-13-million-hack-blamed-on-web-host] Decrypt reports the attacker's account was first funded from Tornado Cash minutes before it was set up. [https://decrypt.co/383732/wrapped-monero-token-collapses-99-after-13-million-hack-blamed-on-web-host]

Wagyu's pseudonymous founder, PerpetualCow, wrote on X that the deployer key sat on an old server at 1984.hosting that he had deleted, Decrypt reports. [https://decrypt.co/383732/wrapped-monero-token-collapses-99-after-13-million-hack-blamed-on-web-host] He pledged to refund losses personally and relaunch, with no timeline. [https://decrypt.co/383732/wrapped-monero-token-collapses-99-after-13-million-hack-blamed-on-web-host] Bitquery said chain data alone cannot show whether the key was stolen. [https://decrypt.co/383732/wrapped-monero-token-collapses-99-after-13-million-hack-blamed-on-web-host]

The redemption question is open. Bitquery said it cannot see how much Monero backs the remaining XMR1, because the Monero side of the bridge is not visible. [https://decrypt.co/383732/wrapped-monero-token-collapses-99-after-13-million-hack-blamed-on-web-host] The same deployer account still controls nearly all of ZEC1, Wagyu's Zcash token, according to Decrypt. [https://decrypt.co/383732/wrapped-monero-token-collapses-99-after-13-million-hack-blamed-on-web-host] The Block puts the loss at $13 million, against Decrypt's $13.5 million. [https://theblock.co/news/defi/2026-11-20-wagyus-xmr1-wrapped-monero-plunges-99-after-13-million-hack-founder-promises-refunds-444747][https://decrypt.co/383732/wrapped-monero-token-collapses-99-after-13-million-hack-blamed-on-web-host]

[NEWS] Icelandic host 1984 ehf. suspends all services after a serious cyberattack.
The company and CERT-IS, Iceland's national incident response team, issued a joint statement on 20 November 2026 describing an extensive attack on its infrastructure, DV reports. [https://www.dv.is/frettir/2026/11/20/stodva-thjonustu-timabundid-eftir-alvarlega-tolvuaras-a-innvidi-1984-ehf] Early findings point to a well-organized and technically sophisticated attack, and the company cannot rule out that more systems were affected than confirmed. [https://www.dv.is/frettir/2026/11/20/stodva-thjonustu-timabundid-eftir-alvarlega-tolvuaras-a-innvidi-1984-ehf] DNS and email will return first under special monitoring, while other services stay down until verified, with no restoration date given. [https://www.dv.is/frettir/2026/11/20/stodva-thjonustu-timabundid-eftir-alvarlega-tolvuaras-a-innvidi-1984-ehf] The statement does not mention Wagyu or say whether customer data was taken, Decrypt notes. [https://decrypt.co/383732/wrapped-monero-token-collapses-99-after-13-million-hack-blamed-on-web-host]

[NEWS] Curve discloses a $38,206 theft from its fee burner, and the proceeds ended in XMR1.
Curve says an attacker made 138 unauthorized trades through its CowSwapBurner contract on 10 November 2026, taking 38,205.76 USDC of DAO fee revenue, Analytics Insight reports from Curve's disclosure. [https://analyticsinsight.net/news/curve-finance-loses-usd-38200-in-dao-revenue-contract-exploit] The contract did not compare a submitted order with the executed one, so valid order data authorized unrelated trades; user deposits, lending markets and crvUSD were not affected. [https://crypto.news/how-did-an-attacker-steal-38200-from-curve-finances-fee-burner/][https://analyticsinsight.net/news/curve-finance-loses-usd-38200-in-dao-revenue-contract-exploit] About 37,230 USDC later bought 65.41 XMR1 on Hyperliquid, the token that collapsed on 20 November 2026. [https://analyticsinsight.net/news/curve-finance-loses-usd-38200-in-dao-revenue-contract-exploit][https://decrypt.co/383732/wrapped-monero-token-collapses-99-after-13-million-hack-blamed-on-web-host] A DAO vote to replace the burner with a descending-price auction that checks the order hash was scheduled to close on 20 November 2026; this edition did not confirm the result. [https://analyticsinsight.net/news/curve-finance-loses-usd-38200-in-dao-revenue-contract-exploit]

[NEWS] Lido holds a single on-chain vote on a lending protocol and module upgrades.
Lido's on-chain Vote #207 ran on 18 and 19 November 2026, Crypto Briefing reports. [https://cryptobriefing.com/lido-dao-votes-lido-lend-csmc/] Its headline item adopts Lido Lend, a fork of Morpho Blue, with each lending market requiring its own approval. [https://cryptobriefing.com/lido-dao-votes-lido-lend-csmc/] The same vote packages upgrades to Lido's Curated Module, so a holder cannot back one part and reject the other. [https://cryptobriefing.com/lido-dao-votes-lido-lend-csmc/] This edition did not confirm the outcome. [https://cryptobriefing.com/lido-dao-votes-lido-lend-csmc/]

TECH

[TECH] POL Finance traces one propAMM trade on Ethereum second by second.
Santiago Di Paolo and two POL Finance colleagues posted the study on Ethereum Research on 19 November 2026. [https://ethresear.ch/t/anatomy-of-a-fill-propamms-on-ethereum/26271] In a propAMM, one market maker streams prices to block builders, who write the latest price into the block just before the user's trade. [https://ethresear.ch/t/anatomy-of-a-fill-propamms-on-ethereum/26271] In the swap they traced, the price update was signed 0.8 seconds after the slot began. [https://ethresear.ch/t/anatomy-of-a-fill-propamms-on-ethereum/26271] The maker learns it was filled a median 1.23 seconds after signing, against milliseconds on a centralized exchange, and the authors say that gap is what the spread pays for. [https://ethresear.ch/t/anatomy-of-a-fill-propamms-on-ethereum/26271]

[TECH] A researcher puts an encrypted PIN inside an Ethereum smart account.
Semih Civelek described Encrypted Account on Ethereum Research on 18 November 2026. [https://ethresear.ch/t/encrypted-account-a-pin-that-lives-in-the-contract/26265] The ERC-4337 account stores a PIN encrypted under Zama's threshold FHE, and the contract counts each guess before its result can be decrypted. [https://ethresear.ch/t/encrypted-account-a-pin-that-lives-in-the-contract/26265] Civelek says online guessing is limited to about 12 attempts in 30 days, and a thief's bond doubles with each wrong guess. [https://ethresear.ch/t/encrypted-account-a-pin-that-lives-in-the-contract/26265] The design is an unaudited research prototype on the Sepolia test network. [https://ethresear.ch/t/encrypted-account-a-pin-that-lives-in-the-contract/26265]
